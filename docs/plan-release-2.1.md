## Decisions

62. **Markers.** `[assumed]`: in-flight delta only; excluded from check 3; never folded (`apply` errors if one remains); each remaining instance is an error under `--require evidence`, message `assumption unresolved; accept it as a claim, mark it [observed] with a test, delete it, or move it to the spec's open questions`. A spec claims part containing `[assumed]` is a check 4 error. `[observed]`: ref required; folds; check 2 wording `characterised behaviour changed; confirm the change is intended`. `[intent]` unchanged. Markers are mutually exclusive on one claim; two is an error. Check 4 claim identity ignores `[observed]` and `[assumed]` as it ignores `[intent]`.
63. **Provenance.** `@per <citation>` is an indented line under a claim, any number. Parsed like `@ref`. A citation matching a decision id resolves when that decision is `accepted` at the range's base; the preview shows `provenance: DECISION-003 (accepted)`. A decision id not accepted at the base shows `(not accepted at base)`. Any other text shows as `(unverified)`. A normative claim with no `@per` shows `provenance: none; accepting this delta establishes this behaviour`. `@per` is never required. Check 4 claim identity ignores `@per` lines, as it ignores `@ref`.
64. **Review surface.** `lemma apply <delta>` preview, and the `in-progress → complete` gate findings in `--hook` mode, print per target: `New commitments`, `Observed behaviour`, `Resolved assumptions`, `Discarded assumptions`, `Unresolved assumptions`. Resolution is derived from the range's commits, not the base: the union of ids that were `[assumed]` in any committed version of the claims part within the range (`git log -p` on the part), minus those `[assumed]` now, is resolved; an id in that union that is gone is discarded. A resolved id kept in place prints `AC-17: was assumed, now a commitment`; one replaced prints `A-1 → AC-17`. In `--hook` mode the surface is one finding per claim (`AC-17: new commitment; provenance: none; accepting this delta establishes this behaviour`), warning severity, emitted at the `in-progress → complete` gate only. The `<n> of <m> claims are [intent]` finding is removed from all output modes.
65. **Entry gate.** `--require intent` replaces `--require claims`: `Intent` present and non-empty, every fold target resolves, every target has its claims part (empty allowed). The `readiness` "no claims" branch is deleted. The pack hook for `draft → in-progress` runs `lemma check --hook --require intent`. `--require claims` is accepted as an alias for one release and prints a deprecation hint.
66. **Prose.** `build.md` step 1: "Record what is known before code: constraints the work must honour, commitments with `@per` where authority exists, and assumptions as `[assumed]`. Preserve unknowns as you meet them; do not invent an acceptance criterion to make the work specifiable." `adopt.md` step 1: "Claim what would be expensive or surprising to rediscover, as `[observed]`. The suite owns the rest; a spec that mirrors it is worse than an empty one." CHG-9: "Build: the delta records what is known going in and preserves unknowns as assumptions; its claims name the verification as they settle." TEST-1: "Every normative claim is mechanised: a test settles it, or it is marked `[intent]`. An `[assumed]` claim is not yet normative." Delta skill vocabulary and lifecycle updated for the three markers and `@per`; never remove `[assumed]` to clear a gate without one of its four exits.
67. **Dictums.** `.lazyspec.toml` declares `convention` (singleton, `docs/convention`, `draft → accepted`) and `dictum` (`docs/convention`, `parent_type = "convention"`, `draft → accepted`, `staleness = "drift"`). The pack ships `CONVENTION.md` and `DICTUM-001-testing.md`, `DICTUM-002-code-change.md` as seed documents; `.lazyspec/dictums/` goes. The entry skill's dictum-copy step is replaced by "if `docs/convention/` is empty after adopt, copy the pack's seed documents" until lazyspec RFC-076 lands. `World` loads `dictum` documents as claim sites with the dictum as its own governing document; test scope is the dictum's `tests` key with the spec defaults. Check 3 applies as for specs. Check 2 on a dictum claim is an error unless the dictum itself strikes that id and adds a new one within the range (dictums are human-edited, never folded; no delta exit). Check 4's fold rule and the edited-outside-a-fold rule do not apply to dictums; its structural rules (invalid glob, legacy lines) do. A dictum's `governs` is not an area for check 1. The validate hook `types` list adds `dictum`.
68. **Names.** Spec part `behaviour` → `claims`; delta part `behaviour.<spec-id>` → `claims.<spec-id>`. `BEHAVIOUR_PART` and `is_behaviour_part` rename accordingly; `behaviour` is still recognised by `migrate` only. Relationship `constrains` (inverse `constrained-by`) replaces `affects`; edges `decision-constrains-spec` and `dictum-constrains-spec`. `lemma signal` reads `constrains`. Templates: spec `index.md` carries `## Intent`, `## Constraints`, `## Non-goals`, `## Open questions` with one HTML-comment line of guidance each; delta `index.md` carries `## Intent`, `## Approach`, `## Out of scope`; `has_intent` is unchanged (guidance comments do not count as content).
69. **Migrate.** `lemma migrate` additionally renames `behaviour.md` to `claims.md` and `behaviour.<id>.md` to `claims.<id>.md` in every spec and delta bundle, and rewrites `affects:` to `constrains:` in decision frontmatter. Idempotent. Prints each file moved or rewritten.
70. **Version 2.1.0** in crate, plugin and README. README gains a Markers section and a Provenance paragraph; the Claims and refs, Checks, Hooks, Fold and Skills sections are updated.
